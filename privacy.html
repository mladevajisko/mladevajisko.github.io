Privacy Policy for tt2yt

tt2yt is a personal automation used only by its developer.

Data accessed

With the user's consent via Google OAuth, the app accesses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the user's own YouTube channel.

How data is used

The access is used solely to upload videos the user owns. No other use is made of it.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the developer's own private server. No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties, and no data is collected from other people.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
